45 min

48–72h

What AI Does Inside UrLife

PHASE 2 LAUNCH

Idea Evaluator

Analyzes any idea and generates a Viability Score with detailed feedback on market, competition, and feasibility.

Smart Matcher

Matches ideas with people based on skills, ambition level, work style, and complementarity — not just keywords.
